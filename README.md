# Inside Out 2 (2024)

## A. Introduction

*Inside Out 2* is an animated movie produced by Pixar Animation Studios and released by Walt Disney Pictures in 2024. It is the sequel to the original *Inside Out* movie, which shows how emotions affect a person's thoughts and decisions. The movie follows Riley as she experiences new emotions while growing up.

**Genre:** Animation, Comedy, Adventure, Family  
**Release Year:** 2024

## B. Synopsis

The story follows Riley, a 13-year-old girl who is entering her teenage years. Inside her mind, Joy, Sadness, Anger, Fear, and Disgust continue to guide her emotions. However, new emotions suddenly appear, including Anxiety, Envy, Embarrassment, and Ennui. Anxiety tries to control Riley's decisions because she wants Riley to succeed and fit in with her new friends. As the emotions struggle to work together, Riley begins to face challenges in understanding herself. In the end, Riley learns that accepting all her emotions is an important part of growing up.

## C. Characters

The following are the important characters in the movie:

- **Joy** – The cheerful emotion who wants Riley to be happy.
- **Sadness** – The emotion who helps Riley understand her feelings and connect with others.
- **Anxiety** – A new emotion who worries about Riley's future and wants her to be prepared.
- **Anger** – The emotion who becomes frustrated when things do not go as planned.
- **Fear** – The emotion who protects Riley from possible dangers.
- **Disgust** – The emotion who helps Riley avoid unpleasant situations.
- **Envy** – The emotion who makes Riley want things that other people have.
- **Embarrassment** – The emotion who makes Riley feel shy or uncomfortable.
- **Ennui** – The emotion who represents boredom and a lack of interest.

## D. Personal Review

I chose *Inside Out 2* because it is an entertaining movie that also teaches important lessons about emotions and growing up. I can relate to Riley because teenagers also experience different feelings and sometimes struggle to understand themselves.

### Things I Liked About the Movie

1. **The story** – I liked how the movie shows the challenges of growing up and dealing with different emotions.
2. **The characters** – Each emotion has a unique personality that makes the movie enjoyable.
3. **The lesson** – The movie teaches that every emotion is important and that we should accept ourselves.

### Recommendation

I recommend this movie to teenagers, students, and families because it is funny, entertaining, and meaningful. It also helps viewers understand that feeling anxious, sad, or embarrassed is a normal part of life.
